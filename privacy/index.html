Website information

A straightforward approach to privacy.

This preview is designed to help you explore VIA and prepare a project brief without creating an account.

Project brief information

Information entered into the project-brief form is processed in your browser. It is not sent to VIA or saved by an application server. Downloading a brief creates a file on your device. The form is cleared when you leave or reload the page.

Email and external links

If you choose an email link, your email application handles the message. Sending is your choice. External websites, including LinkedIn and project source links, have their own privacy practices.

Analytics and cookies

This version does not use advertising trackers, third-party analytics, or application cookies. GitHub Pages hosts the shared preview and processes technical requests, including IP addresses, to deliver and secure the site. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Confidential information

Please do not include procurement-sensitive material, controlled information, government identifiers, or other confidential documents in a project brief.

Before public launch

Contact details and any future inquiry-delivery service will be reflected here before they are enabled. This information describes the current preview.
