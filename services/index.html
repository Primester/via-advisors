Our services

The right advice. At the right moment.

From a property’s potential to a project’s delivery, we help public owners see the choices, understand the tradeoffs, and move forward.

01

Feasibility & alternatives

Make the right decision before making the commitment.

Understand what a property can support, compare the alternatives, and give decision-makers a practical path forward.

02

Owner’s representation

Keep the owner’s priorities at the center of the project.

Connect the budget, schedule, consultants, and decisions through clear project controls and accountable reporting.

03

Due diligence coordination

Bring the right information together before moving forward.

Organize site investigations and specialist findings into one understandable assessment of the property and its risks.

04

Site & market analysis

Find the fit between a public need and a real place.

Screen locations, compare constraints, and connect program requirements with real estate and market considerations.

05

Lease project support

Translate space requirements into a workable project.

Support requirements, alternatives, and tenant-improvement delivery alongside the agency’s authorized leasing team.

06

Capital & portfolio planning

Put each property decision in the context of the whole.

Prioritize investment, compare reuse options, and connect real property decisions to the organization’s long-term needs.

VIA provides advisory and coordination services. Licensed design, engineering, appraisal, brokerage, and construction services are outside VIA’s own scope and are addressed separately with appropriately qualified professionals.
